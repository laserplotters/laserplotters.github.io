What Is a Laser Plotter?

A laser plotter is a specialized machine that uses laser technology to create precise markings, patterns, outlines, or cutting paths on various materials. Depending on the application, laser plotters can be used for engraving, marking, cutting, drafting, prototyping, textile processing, PCB production, packaging design, and industrial manufacturing.

Compared with traditional plotting methods, laser plotters offer higher precision, faster processing speeds, and greater flexibility when handling complex designs.
